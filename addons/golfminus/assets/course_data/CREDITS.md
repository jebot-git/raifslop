# Course geometry and elevation

Mapped routing, playing-surface outlines and coastline: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL): https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . The derived course databases (courses/spyglass.json, courses/pebble.json, courses/cypress.json, courses/poppy.json and assets/course_data) are offered under ODbL. Authoring inputs and the reproducible conversion script are retained in source/course_references and tools/build_reference_courses.py.

Regional terrain elevations: Copernicus DEM 2021 GLO-90, obtained through Open-Meteo (https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/elevation-api), https://doi.org/10.5270/ESA-c5d3d65 . © European Union / Copernicus programme. Attribution: Copernicus / Open-Meteo.

Official course maps and scorecards were consulted for identification and routing checks; their artwork is not distributed as game textures. See each course's layout.reference metadata for the URLs.

This is a mapped terrain adaptation, not a surveyed replica. Green grades, bunker depth, individual trees and clubhouse architecture are approximations. Original fictional-course saved rounds and records retain their original IDs; the new courses have separate records.

Continuous playing lanes are gameplay adaptations generated by `tools/build_course_lanes.py`. The original OSM routes and surface outlines remain available; `routing.play_path`, `routing.tee_paths` and `outlines.play_lanes` describe added fairway connections and hazard detours. Tree canopies are cleared around these lanes. Terrain heights, mapped water and bunkers are preserved.

Additional courses (22 September 2026): Cypress Point Club and Poppy Hills Golf Course. OpenStreetMap API extracts and 100 Open-Meteo elevation samples per course are archived under source/course_references. Cypress Point identification was checked against the USGA hole tour (https://video.usga.org/videos/2025/08/condoleezza-rice-narrates-every-hole-at-cypress-po-6377527661112.html); Poppy Hills against its NCGA course tour and scorecard (https://poppyhillsgolf.ncga.org/course-tour and https://poppyhillsgolf.ncga.org/scorecard). The game uses mapped club-course pars rather than a particular tournament setup. Reference artwork is not distributed.
