# Equipment materials

Clubhouse timber derives from [Ash Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/ash_veneer) by Jenelle van Heerden, Poly Haven. The warm oiled albedo is a tinted derivative.

Foundation slate uses [Castle Wall Slates](https://polyhaven.com/a/castle_wall_slates), Poly Haven.

Both texture sets were downloaded at 2K through Blender MCP / Poly Haven and are [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Original albedo, OpenGL normal and roughness maps are retained here.

Carbon twill, moulded rubber, brushed metal and powdercoat maps were generated procedurally in Blender by source/finish_equipment.py for Golf Minus.
