Hybrid Splat Viewer 0.1.0-splat.1 — testing prerelease

Two environment areas: Lake Pier and Simons Rocks.
This is a scenery viewer, with no fishing gameplay, multiplayer or voice chat.

Linux / Windows:
  Desktop launcher: keyboard and mouse.
  VR launcher: start your OpenXR runtime and connect your headset first.
  Linux WiVRn launcher: select the installed WiVRn runtime for this process.
  1 / 2: Lake Pier / Simons Rocks
  WASD: walk; hold right mouse and drag: look
  B: splats on/off; L: Simons old/new lighting
  R: reset position; P: save screenshot and camera pose; Escape: quit

VR / Quest / Pico (Touch-style button names):
  Left stick: walk; right stick: 30-degree snap turn
  Right A: switch location; right B: splats on/off
  Headset tracking supplies your view; the collision capsule follows room movement.
  Exit using the headset's application menu.

Settings, captures and diagnostic reports are separate from the fishing game.
Linux: ~/.local/share/RealAIFishing-SplatTesting/
Windows: %APPDATA%\RealAIFishing-SplatTesting\
Android: private app data for org.jebot.raifslop.splattesting.quest or .pico
Saved settings: last area, splat visibility and Simons lighting choice.
Screenshots and reports: captures/ inside that directory.
No saves are imported from or written to the main game.

Known limits: remaining shoreline reconstruction artifacts; two-area prototype.
Linux desktop runtime tested. Windows and Android builds are export/pack audited.
New headset and standalone-device visual/performance validation is still pending.
