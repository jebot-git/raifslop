## Legal-basis rationale and limits

The notice identifies legitimate interests in delivering the features players
use, testing gameplay, troubleshooting and responding to support. These purposes
require the disclosed state, temporary voice/pose buffers, server records and
voluntarily submitted messages. Independent local play, optional-feature
controls, pseudonymous identity, short testing retention and free deletion
reduce interference with users' rights. Persistent identifiers, minors' data,
shared avatar files, default voice activation and unencrypted direct transport
remain relevant risks; a privacy notice does not remove them. Do not expand
processing without reviewing necessity, safeguards and users' expectations.
The notice uses legal obligation for statutory requests and other legal duties.
It does not claim that existing permission prompts establish legal consent.

This is the documented drafting rationale, not a legal opinion or a completed
regulatory assessment. Applicability of Serbian/EEA/UK law, representative
requirements and any required international-transfer instruments depend on
actual distribution and provider arrangements. Cloudzy's public notice does
not establish a specific customer DPA or transfer instrument. No such agreement,
Serbian adequacy status, or blanket GDPR compliance is asserted. Confirm any
required contractual arrangements with the provider for the launch regions.
