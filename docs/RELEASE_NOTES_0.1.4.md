Real AI Fishing 0.1.4 adds two coastal waters, eight marine fish and more responsive fights.

- Coastal Rocks has a rocky shoreline with rope barriers; Sunrise Beach has a walkable sand shore. Both use native 8K HDR panoramas, recorded surf and marine-only catch rosters.
- Eight textured marine species: blacktail, galjoen, hottentot, red roman, white steenbras, elf/bluefish, Cape yellowtail and harder mullet. Saltwater baits, tackle visuals and guide entries switch with the location.
- Stronger feedback while correctly countering a fish, reduced feedback when failing to counter, left-hand reeling feedback and a subtle water-ripple sound when a fight or escape attempt starts.
- Submerging fish can rapidly pull tension up or introduce slack, requiring a pause or faster reeling.
- Caught fish twitch subtly. Reconstructed fish fins are attached and catch lengths are checked against the enforced 170 cm avatar height.
- Lake Pier ground meets the lowered water. Lakeside and Gray Pier use grassy ground and rope barriers; location rope has a uniform tan colour. Shore insects are smaller and much sparser.

Existing freshwater catch indices and Android signing identity are retained. Update multiplayer clients and servers together so all participants have the new locations and species.

Downloads include Linux and Windows desktop/VR/server archives and separate Quest/Pico APKs. Desktop HDR uses BC6H compression; Android retains lossless HDR. Textures are deduplicated and archives use maximum DEFLATE compression. Documentation and authoring files stay in the repository; asset credits and licenses are included.

Windows execution and standalone Quest/Pico play and performance require device validation. Automated simulations and desktop checks do not verify physical haptic feel.
