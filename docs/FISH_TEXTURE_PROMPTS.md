# Fish texture generation

Mode: OpenAI built-in `image_gen` tool, not the API/CLI fallback. Outputs retained in the project:

- `source/textures/fish/tench_albedo.png`
- `source/textures/fish/roach_albedo.png`

The images are generated photographic-style references, not photographs of actual fish. They are projected onto full 3D bodies and separate fin meshes in Blender. No online reference photograph is embedded or copied into the assets.

## Tench — exact prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: precise lateral texture reference for a realistic 3D freshwater tench fish in a fishing game. Create a high resolution 1536x1024 landscape image showing exactly ONE real adult TENCH (Tinca tinca), full body, perfect orthographic side profile, snout pointing RIGHT and tail LEFT. Fish fills 90 percent image width with generous empty space above and below. Neutral solid white background, no water, no ground, no cast shadow, no text. Anatomically accurate robust stocky tench, NOT carp, NOT bream: rounded blunt fleshy snout and lips, small orange-red iris with round black pupil, short single barbel visible at mouth corner, fine densely packed tiny embedded olive-bronze scales, very dark moss olive green back grading naturally to olive gold flanks and pale cream-yellow belly, subtle wet mucus sheen and irregular natural pigmentation, detailed realistic gill cover and lateral line. ONE low rounded soft dorsal fin near middle back, rounded fleshy pectoral and pelvic fins, short rounded anal fin and broad almost square lightly concave unforked tail. All fins fully visible and gently spread, thin translucent grey-olive brown membranes and very fine natural fin rays. True photographic surface detail, natural proportions and restrained colors, no cartoon styling, no polygonal appearance, no oversized eye, no thick outlines, no enormous scales. Flat evenly diffuse neutral lighting specifically for texture projection: retain detailed scale and fin patterns with minimal baked shading or directional specular highlights. The dorsal and belly silhouette must be unoccluded. It should look like a high-quality fisheries specimen photograph.

## Roach — exact prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: orthographic lateral photographic texture for a realistic 3D common roach fish, Rutilus rutilus. Create a 1536x1024 landscape image of exactly one anatomically correct mature COMMON ROACH, full unobstructed side profile, snout pointing RIGHT, tail LEFT, filling 90 percent of image width. Pure solid white background, no shadow, no text, no setting. This must look like a real fisheries specimen photograph, not a drawing, toy, stylized model or illustration. Slender moderately deep laterally compressed silvery freshwater cyprinid body, blue-grey olive back, pearly silver flanks with clearly resolved overlapping natural cycloid scales, discreet lateral line, pale silver belly. Small head with forward terminal mouth and a small natural red-orange iris around round black pupil, eye diameter about 2 percent of total fish length, NO oversized cartoon eye. Natural thin grey dorsal fin with subdued reddish-brown rays, strongly forked grey-red tail, reddish orange pelvic and anal fins and pale reddish pectoral fins. All fins spread and fully visible, fine translucent fin membranes with natural detailed rays, no barbels. Natural slightly irregular scale pattern and realistic gill cover. Even diffuse soft neutral lighting for texture projection, minimal directional highlights and minimal baked shading. Sharp photographic focus over the whole fish. Restrained realistic coloration, metallic silver skin rather than yellow gold, no ink outlines or plastic surface.
