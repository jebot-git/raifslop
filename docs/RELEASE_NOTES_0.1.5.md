# Real AI Fishing 0.1.5

## Fishing

- Fly fishing on two new river waters: Meadow Bend and Boulder Run. Dry flies and nymphs, current-driven drift, line mending, stripping and a small strike indicator for nymphs.
- Rare predator takeovers: Wels catfish inland and bronze whaler sharks at sea, with a 2% chance on eligible smaller hooked fish. A loud splash announces the takeover; opening runs demand a quick stop to reeling and careful tension management.
- Species-specific fight patterns, endurance and run/dive behavior. Experienced anglers can learn broader fish families from their fights.
- Hooked fish become visible just below the surface near landing. Eligible trout can jump; a rapid sideways tug against the jump drains stamina, while a missed counter lets the fish recover.
- New predator models, sizes, guide entries and journal records.

## Environments

- Textured river banks, layered crossed shrubs and alders, with shrub bases grounded across their footprints.
- Lakeside shrub and ground-cover transitions, Gray Pier reed cutouts, and low granite formations behind Simon's Town's rear rope.
- Subdued vegetation brightness matched to each panorama, clamped cutout edges and removal of overlapping old vegetation and detached reed heads.

Update multiplayer clients and servers together for the new locations, fish and fight behavior.

## Packages

Linux and Windows x86_64 archives, Quest and Pico ARM64 APKs, notices, build manifest and SHA256 checksums. Android version code 6; the existing release signing identity is retained.

Native 8K panoramas remain intact. Desktop HDR compression, identical-texture deduplication and level-9 archive/APK compression are retained. Editable sources, test captures and development tools stay outside the game packages.

## Validation and limits

Gameplay, fish models, river drift, predator takeovers, jump counters, scenery grounding and networking are checked before publication. The exported Linux pack and all packaged runtime assets are checked; Android signatures, ARM64 content and 16 KiB alignment are verified.

Windows and standalone Quest/Pico execution are not tested on the build host. New cutout scenery and fly-fishing controller feel still need on-headset validation; distant rock cutouts have limited depth at extreme side angles.
